Best Smartphones Under ₦200,000 in Nigeria (2025)

GearNest Editorial ·May 20, 2025 ·8 min read

The Nigerian smartphone market in 2025 is more competitive than ever. With the naira stabilizing somewhat and brands like Infinix, Tecno, and Xiaomi aggressively targeting the mid-range segment, there has never been a better time to get serious smartphone performance without emptying your bank account.

After hands-on testing and extensive research into prices at major Nigerian retailers including Slot, Pointek, and online platforms like Jumia and Konga, here are the best smartphones you can buy under ₦200,000 in Nigeria right now.

1. Infinix NOTE 40 Pro+ — Best Overall (₦185,000)

The Infinix NOTE 40 Pro+ is our top pick in this price range, and it's not particularly close. Infinix has absolutely nailed this device. You get a stunning 6.78-inch AMOLED 144Hz display that looks genuinely premium, a 108MP main camera that produces impressive shots in good lighting, and — most remarkably — both 100W wired and wireless charging support.

The Helio G99 Ultimate processor handles everyday tasks, social media, and even some gaming with ease. Our battery life tests showed the 5000mAh cell lasting nearly two full days of moderate use. At ₦185,000, the NOTE 40 Pro+ represents extraordinary value.

  • Best for: Power users who want flagship features on a budget
  • Display: 6.78" AMOLED, 144Hz refresh rate
  • Camera: 108MP main + AI-enhanced portrait mode
  • Battery: 5000mAh with 100W fast charging

2. Realme GT 6T 5G — Best for Gaming (₦195,000)

If mobile gaming is a priority, the Realme GT 6T 5G is the phone to beat under ₦200k. Powered by the Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 — a chip that punches well above its weight class — this phone handles graphics-intensive games like PUBG Mobile and Genshin Impact at high settings without breaking a sweat.

The 144Hz AMOLED display is smooth and responsive, making gaming feel incredibly fluid. The 5500mAh battery with 120W charging means you can top up from zero to full in under 20 minutes. For gamers in Nigeria, this is a no-brainer.

3. Tecno CAMON 20 Pro 5G — Best Camera (₦195,000–₦210,000)

Tecno has quietly built one of the best camera phones in the sub-₦250,000 segment. The CAMON 20 Pro 5G features a 64MP RGBW sensor — a unique setup that captures more light than traditional RGB sensors, resulting in noticeably brighter and cleaner low-light photos.

If you're someone who documents life constantly — events, outings, content creation — the CAMON 20 Pro 5G delivers results that would embarrass phones costing twice as much.

Verdict

The sub-₦200,000 segment in Nigeria has never been this strong. Whether you prioritize battery life, gaming, or photography, there's a device that delivers genuine flagship-adjacent performance at a fraction of the cost. Our top recommendation remains the Infinix NOTE 40 Pro+ for most users.

Always buy from authorized retailers to ensure warranty coverage. Check Slot, Pointek, or official brand stores for the most reliable purchasing experience.
